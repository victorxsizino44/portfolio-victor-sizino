## 2. Purpose

After evaluating the fit of the current positioning and the accumulated technical trajectory, Victor decided to reposition his primary public identity toward frontend engineering, retaining Full-Stack, AI Product Engineering, product and leadership as complementary capabilities.

Canonical facts and professional projections remain distinct. Employment, official titles, annual chronology and approved evidence remain governed by Phase 01. This baseline changes how approved facts may be prioritized in future projections; it does not change the underlying facts or rewrite the historical decision record.

The objective is to restore engineering as the dominant professional reading while preserving product, AI, leadership and international experience as differentiators. No public copy, code, CV, LinkedIn profile, agent, asset or production environment is changed or authorized by this baseline.

## 3. Strategic Positioning

Approved complete positioning:

> **Senior Front-End Developer | Full-Stack & AI Product Engineering**

Approved short English form:

> Senior Front-End Developer with full-stack capabilities and experience building AI-powered digital products.

Approved Portuguese form:

Narrative hierarchy:

1. Frontend Engineering;
2. Full-Stack capabilities;
3. AI Product Engineering;
4. technical leadership;
5. product and Technical Product Management;
6. stakeholders and international experience.

Frontend must be the first reading. React, Next.js, TypeScript and JavaScript form the center of the technical proposition. Full-Stack means demonstrable end-to-end capability and low-to-medium-complexity backend work, not backend specialization equivalent to frontend.

AI Product Engineering means integrating external AI services and models into usable digital products, including interfaces, contracts, server-side flows, validation, failure handling, security and governance. It does not imply model training, fine-tuning, ML engineering, data science, MLOps, RAG, vector databases or authorship of provider models.
